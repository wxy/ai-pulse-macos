## English

AI Pulse for iPhone is the read-only companion to AI Pulse for Mac. It brings the facts already captured on your Mac to your iPhone and Apple Watch without turning separate signals into a made-up bill.

See Today, This Week, and 30 Days as independent snapshots. Review captured token activity, tool and model shares, authorized-repository activity, local Git changes, and current activity status. Where the source makes them available, balance and quota observations retain their original units and timestamps. Missing or stale observations are not shown as healthy zeroes.

The iPhone widget shows today's pulse at a glance. The Apple Watch app and complications provide compact views of the three rings, current activity, Tokens, and Lines. Tokens and Lines are stable metric labels across languages.

AI Pulse for Mac remains the data source. It parses supported local tool logs and configured Git repositories, then sends derived summaries through your private iCloud database when sync is enabled. API keys are not included in those dashboard payloads. Availability depends on the source tools, permissions, account state, and recent Mac synchronization.

Requirements:

- AI Pulse for Mac, installed and configured
- The same Apple Account and private iCloud container on your devices
- iPhone with iOS 16 or later; Apple Watch features require watchOS 10 or later
