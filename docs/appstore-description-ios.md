## English

### Description

**AI Pulse for iOS is the companion to the macOS app.** It only reads and displays, on your iPhone or iPad, the AI usage and spending information collected on your Mac — today, this week, and 30-day trends, a donut chart of your AI costs by tool and provider, and per-repo breakdowns. Check your AI spend on the go, straight from your pocket.

Please install and configure the **macOS version first** — that's where your AI usage is collected, then synced to this app through iCloud. Your data stays only on your own device, your iCloud, and your own clients. It is never uploaded anywhere else.

💡 **Universal Purchase — buy once, use on iOS and macOS.**
A single purchase covers both platforms. Already own the Mac version but still see a price button on your iPhone or iPad? Don't worry — as long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID, just tap the price button and the App Store will recognize your existing purchase and let you download for free. You will never be charged twice.

**At a Glance**
- Today / This Week / 30 Days — your AI spending at a glance
- Donut chart — cost breakdown by AI tool and provider
- Tool & repo ranking — which projects and AI tools cost the most
- Push notifications — spending alerts with coin sound effects
- iCloud sync — automatic, zero configuration

**Privacy**
- Read-only companion — this app never collects or uploads anything
- iCloud sync happens only between your own devices signed in with the same Apple ID
- Your AI usage never leaves your Apple ID ecosystem

**Requirements**
- iPhone or iPad with iOS 16 or later
- The macOS version of AI Pulse, installed and configured (data source)
- Signed in with the same Apple ID on all your devices

请务必先下载并配置 **macOS 版**——AI 用量信息由 macOS 版采集，通过 iCloud 同步到本应用。这些数据仅保存在您的本地设备、您的 iCloud 以及您自己的客户端中，绝不会上传到其他任何地方。

💡 **多平台通用购买（Universal Purchase）—— 一次购买，iOS 与 macOS 通用。**

**环境要求**
